Refund Eligibility

Due to the nature of digital educational content, refunds are not generally available once access to courses, test series, study materials, mock tests, recorded lectures, or subscriptions has been granted.

Duplicate Transactions

If multiple payments are accidentally processed for the same order, the additional amount may be refunded after successful verification.

Failed Transactions

If payment is deducted but service access is not provided, our team will verify the transaction and either activate access or initiate a refund where applicable.

Refund Processing Timeline

Approved refunds are generally processed within 7–14 business days. Processing time may vary depending on banks and payment providers.

Subscription Cancellation

Users may cancel recurring subscriptions before the next billing cycle. Cancellation prevents future charges but does not guarantee a refund for current or previous billing periods.

Non-Refundable Cases

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Failure to use purchased content.
  • Downloaded study materials.
  • Completed courses or test series.
  • Violation of platform policies.
  • Promotional or discounted purchases unless otherwise stated.

Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes become effective immediately upon publication on this page.
